# Wanderer

Immersion and comfort for your journey through Azeroth, made for **World of Warcraft Forever**.

Names leave the heads of everyone around you and a label appears right above someone when you look at them. Conversations with NPCs become quiet scenes, every item tells you whether it is better for the branch you play, your unit frames take a clean look, and your interface steps aside when nothing happens. The small comforts you would install a dozen addons for (junk sold, repairs, provisions refilled on your click, instant loot, double-click fishing, reminders, your characters in one window, movable windows...) are gathered in one.

- **Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/wanderer)
- **Website:** [wanderer-addon.com](https://wanderer-addon.com)
- **Trailer:** [YouTube](https://youtu.be/iptaXzImWI4)
- **Options:** Esc > Options > AddOns > Wanderer, or type `/wanderer`

Everything comes pre-set and can be changed, each character has its own profile, holding Shift keeps every automation manual, nothing decides for you in a fight, and every game setting Wanderer touches is given back when you turn it off.

**Works with your other addons.** When ElvUI, DialogueUI, Leatrix Plus, Naowh Forever or another addon already does a job, Wanderer steps aside and its option says who does it. Compatible with **Total RP 3** and **Spoken** (its voices play in Wanderer's scenes).

**The Wanderer family.** Free extensions, each a separate download: Wanderer Bags, Auction, Quests, Campfire Game, Training, Chat, Mail, Maps and Outfits.

Translated into English, Français, Deutsch, Español, Italiano, Português (Brasil), Русский, 한국어, 简体中文 and 繁體中文.
