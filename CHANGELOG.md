# Wanderer 0.37.0

### New
- The fishing told: over the bobber, the label says what happens (the line lands, the wait, the last seconds) with the time left, and a short notice tells each catch, the fish and the count of the session (an option of the fishing). Once hovered, the label stays on the bobber while the line is in the water.
- Reminders: a right click on a line makes it important (a star, on top), moves it up or down, or ties it to the place where you stand: back there, a notice says what is left to do, and the game's own map pin shows the way.
- At a dungeon's door: a short notice tells the quests of your log done there and, when it is worn, the state of your equipment.
- Back in town with very worn equipment, a short notice: a smith would be welcome (an option of the merchants).
- The travel journal keeps your memories: each screenshot is written with the place; each day of the chronicle shows what it held (places, quests, catches, crafts).
- Private messages: a search over the people, by name or by a word of the conversation.
- In a fight, the label no longer blinks over a character that the game loses and finds again for an instant (it moves under the mouse), nor when it is your target too.
- Enemies' names, a choice of the names page: as your names settings say, always, never, or as you come near (out of a fight, an enemy's health bar, or its name alone, appears as you approach, faint then clear, faded by the game with the distance; the distance is yours to set).
- The level colors follow the game exactly (a creature three levels under you is green, as on the target frame).
- The game tooltip hidden by Wanderer takes its health bar with it (it blinked alone in a fight).
- An enemy's level in the color of its difficulty in the target block of the label too, and in the game's tooltip over the frames (your target, your group).
- Web addresses in the chat become links: a click shows the address ready to copy.
- An innkeeper's label tells where your home is (here or elsewhere) and whether your hearthstone is ready.
- The ways to play of the welcome screen set the enemies' names too (as you come near for Immersion and Roleplay).
- The options: the Adventure page in two, Journal and session, Fishing and professions.
- Mailbox: a "Take the gold" button takes only the gold of the letters; their items, the letters without gold and those asking for payment stay.
- Travel mode: moving the mouse no longer brings the interface back during a flight; it comes back only while the mouse is over one of its parts. The destination stays longer (12 seconds).
- What the label says, in three levels (essential, complete, all of it); each line can still be set alone.
- The label hidden in a fight (option) hides the game's tooltip over the characters too: a clear screen.
- The fishing journal: a page of the travel journal keeps every fish caught (where first, how many), the first of each species written in the chronicle; a fine catch is told in its color, a new species and a streak are greeted.
- A pole without a lure is said on the bobber.
- Hear the splash (option, off): while the line is in the water, the music and the ambience step back, then come back as they were.
- Professions: each point gained said by a short notice (several in a row: one), the first craft of each recipe written in the journal, and the session summary tells what you gathered (herbs, ore, leather, fish).

### Fixed
- Marking an enemy from the wheel works when it is not your target, and the label fades above a head instead of vanishing.
- A nameplate given to another character no longer shows the previous one's name for a moment.
- Merchant: nothing is sold or used once the window is closed.
- Mailbox: a letter the game refuses (a unique item, the gold cap) is passed over instead of being tried again and again; the gold told is the gold received.
- The travel journal writes nothing while it is off, and a reload no longer cuts a play session in two.
- Camera: after a reload during a flight or a conversation it comes back to your distance; the settings borrowed for conversations and the away ambiance are given back when you log out.
- A conversation never opens as a fight starts, hiding the interface (Alt+Z) no longer ends it, and the interface no longer flashes back right after it.
- Chat: lines the game keeps secret (restricted places) pass untouched; Battle.net lines are not kept in a tab's history; with ElvUI's chat history on, lines are not shown twice; the chat's arrow keys are left to the game and other addons when Wanderer's option is off.
- After a fight, the private messages open on the conversation with unread messages.
- A group invitation accepted for you is no longer declined by its closing window.
- Profiles: resetting or deleting a place's profile no longer changes the character's own; the game's Defaults button sets two settings to their right value; a game setting that cannot be put back during a fight is put back at the next load.
- An option turned off during a fight (minimap icons, moved windows) applies when it ends.
- Professions and the fishing double click keep working when the game lacks one of its functions; a full reminders list keeps the line you typed.
