# Wanderer 0.38.0

### New
- A power bar (mana, rage, energy, focus) in the game's colors under the health bar: in the label, in its target block and in the game's tooltip over the frames (an option of the label's content).
- Private messages: a button at the right of the game's social button, above the chat, drawn like it, opens them, the count of what is unread under it (0 included), a yellow bubble on it; it glows softly when a new whisper comes.
- Your guild in the world: each member's name small and faint over their head, in the game's color for your guild (an option of the names page, beside your group's).
- One text size for every chat window (16 by default), new windows included: the game sets it window by window. Turning Wanderer off gives each window its size back.
- Web addresses in the guild's windows (its information, its message of the day) become links too, ready to copy.
- Blessings to cast again: when one of your long blessings (Arcane Intellect, Mark of the Wild, Fortitude...) fades from you or your group, out of a fight, a short notice says which and on whom (an option of the group page).
- Better or worse for your branch, like Pawn: over an item you could wear, a line says how it compares with what you wear there, for the branch you play ("Frost: +12 % better"), from the characteristics each branch values (an option of the tooltips).
- Better or worse: a feral druid sees two lines, the bear's and the cat's; scales of Pawn (Raidbots, guides) can be imported to replace Wanderer's weights for a class, one line each.
- Private messages: the color of each side's bubbles, to choose.
- The Stacked and Clean layouts show the game's swing timer small, in fights only, over the cast bar (turned on for the classes that strike).
- The weights window (options of the tooltips, or /wanderer weights): what each characteristic is worth to you, scale by scale, set with the wheel or - and +, a gauge beside each; Wanderer's weights become yours as soon as one moves and come back with Restore; a scale exported or imported as Pawn writes it.

### Changed
- The options sorted by use: Every day (reminders, merchants, loot, trainers, fishing, professions), Group (threat, blessings, the dungeon's door), Tooltips and items; each window a button away where it is set.
- The minimap button's menu: the reminders, the private messages, the item weights, then the photo mode, the settings and the help; a right click opens the reminders.
- A small button in the character sheet's title bar opens the weights.
- In a fight the label says only what matters for it (level, rank, health and power, the spell cast, whom it looks at); its other lines come back after (an option, on).
- The label no longer stays on your target during a fight: it shows only what you hover, and comes back on your target once the fight is over.
- The ways to play of the welcome screen set the new settings too: the guild's names, the power bar (Adventurer and Dungeons), the splash heard while fishing (Immersion and Roleplay), whispers as conversations (Roleplay).
- Private messages: a window of its own; the game's windows (the spellbook...) and Escape no longer close it, the interface fades around it, and it stays open in a fight, to answer if you wish.
- Flights: the destination card stays all the way, discreet after its first seconds, and goes when you land.
- Private messages: hovering a person now only says that a right click gives more options.
- Private messages: who the person is fits on two lines, their room kept from the start (what you looked up is no longer cut, and nothing jumps when it comes), and the window is resized by its corner, its size kept.

### Fixed
- After a look at someone from the private messages, your own /who answers in the chat again.
- Back from an ambiance while away, the camera returns to your distance even when it was still moving.

### Removed
- The travel journal, the session summary and the rares' victories on the label: little used, too heavy for what they gave. What they had written stays in the saved file, untouched.
