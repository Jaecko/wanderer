# Wanderer 1.4.101

### New
- Help and hints in every window are a small card of their own: a title, short lines with air between them, the key words picked out, at the size of words chosen in the options and in the windows' look.
- A choice for Wanderer's windows, under Appearance and in the welcome screen's Look step: the game's own look, or Wanderer's (the default). The game's look reaches every part of the windows: the game's checkboxes (for the switches too), its lists to choose from, its options' tabs, its thin scroll bars, its lines to write in (the three money boxes too), its sunken panels, its item squares framed in their quality's color, its question window, and the game's own colors for titles, words and hints. Rows lit and chosen as in the game's options. The pieces of the screen (the bar of links, the loot recap, the objectives, the chat) keep Wanderer's colors in either look.

### Fixed
- Group loot: a roll whose item the game had not read yet no longer hides the next roll's buttons, and the appearance roll is named once chosen.
- The minimap button no longer keeps following the mouse when it is hidden in the middle of a drag.
- A new merchant opens at the top of his goods, not where the last one was left.
- The reveal key let go while Wanderer was turned off no longer leaves every name shown.
- With a controller: a box to tick or a round choice is one stop, not two; a list to choose from opens Wanderer's own card (the game's menu could block its actions), and closes with its window; switching windows with the triggers brings the chosen one to the front.
- The game's look: the resize corner, the cog and the "?" are drawn over the frame's edges.

### Changed
- Group rolls: once you have chosen, the card's other choices are greyed until the roll ends (a second click no longer sends a second roll).
- With a controller: the merchant's "Repair one item" steps aside (it needs a mouse; "Repair all" stays), and the keys' page and the welcome's reveal key say a key is set with a keyboard.
