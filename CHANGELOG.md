# Wanderer 0.39.0

### New
- Wanderer now welcomes extensions: each has its own page in the options (before the profiles), follows your profiles and draws its windows in Wanderer's style.
- The first one, Wanderer Bags (a separate download on CurseForge and Wago), puts your bags and your bank in one Wanderer window, with Wanderer's green arrow on what is better for your branch.
