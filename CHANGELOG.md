# Wanderer 1.4.105

### New
- A class assistant under your frame: your class's spell icons with their countdown and charges, as on your action bars. Poisons and Slice and Dice, totems, seals and auras, armors, ammo, soul shards, healthstones, mana gems and water, Inner Fire, shouts and stances. In a fight what matters then; at rest only what is missing, ending or running low (an orange edge, a dimmed icon). Never a spell to cast, never a sound.
- A spark on your power bar: the 2 second energy tick, or the 5 second rule on your mana.
- Druids in bear or cat form keep their mana in sight, a third bar under the others.
- A hunter's pet mood beside its frame, orange when it wants feeding.
- All under Unit frames, Class assistant, on for everyone.

### Fixed
- With another unit frame addon (ElvUI...), the class assistant keeps its row where your frame stands.
- In a fight, a weapon's poison or oil whose time is hidden stays shown.
- A list's menu closes with its window, with the mouse too.
- With a controller, a list's menu closes with its window instead of staying on screen.
