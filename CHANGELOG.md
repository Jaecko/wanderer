# Wanderer 1.4.0

### New
- Your unit frames in Wanderer's look: your frame, target, focus, party, bosses, raid-style group frames and cast bars, in four looks (Fine, Ink lines, Classic, As the game); portraits as medallions with a ring in class or attitude color. The game's code, values and Edit Mode sizes stay untouched.
- What your damage over time will still deal (a band and a filling skull) and what your heals over time will still heal (a deep green band and a plus), on frames and nameplates. Information only, off by default.
- Threat at a glance in a group, "Not yours yet" over a monster nobody has hit, and your layer in one line of the zone's tooltip by the minimap.
- The players you know keep a small faint name over their head (grouped with, whispered, friends, or "Remember this name" from their menu), and your guild's members keep theirs in the guild's color.
- Wanderer's card: a left click on the crest or the bar shows your profile, the names, what you changed, a tile for every window, recent notices, placing and photo.
- Hold the reveal key and the faded interface comes back with the names, until you let go.
- Calm movements: one setting and Wanderer never moves the camera (scenes, travels, away) and its pieces fade in place without sliding.
- The first time the camera moves for a scene or a flight, a notice says so, with a click to leave the camera where it is.
- Conversation scenes: the choices and quests you already read show dimmed, never hidden; the panel can be placed where you like and its background opacity set.
- Scenes: a reward chosen then taken with Space, a turn-in costing gold never paid by accident, what was said one wheel away, and Spoken's voices followed sentence by sentence with "Listen again".
- A reading window for books, plaques and letters, your page remembered.
- Provisions: what you rebuy is learned, and "Fill up" at the merchant buys what is missing, on your click only.
- The sell price in every tooltip, the herbs or ores a new gathering rank brings within reach, a word whispered to you that invites, a click on a campfire that seats you beside it.
- Your characters: one window with each character's level, rested experience, gold, professions, cooldowns and letters about to leave.
- Each character has its own profile and its own profiles per place (world, cities, dungeons), a welcome of its own, and can start "Like" your main.
- Edit Mode layouts made in one click, action bars 2 to 8 shown or hidden, lighter slots, a text-only look for the damage meter.
- Plays well with other addons: when DialogueUI, Naowh Forever, ElvUI, Leatrix Plus or another addon already does a job, Wanderer steps aside and its option says who does it. Total RP 3 can keep its own card.
- For the extensions: API 17, the shared kit of windows, buttons and words, the account's characters.

### Changed
- A new finish everywhere: windows open in a breath, Wanderer's own signs and question window, your font on every button and title, the options in the order of the eye with a search and a "What you changed" page.
- Every setting comes pre-set and stays yours to change; a setting changed in town now holds everywhere.
- In a dungeon or a raid the camera stays still during scenes (a group waits), and the away ambiance never starts in a group or flagged for PvP out of a resting place.
- On a flight, the chat fades with the rest of the interface.

### Removed
- The chat moves to Wanderer Chat, and the mailbox to Wanderer Mail: two separate extensions.

### Fixed
- The game's damage text over your portrait now shows above the medallion.
- Two characters sharing a first name no longer share their settings, reminders or provisions.
- A fight that interrupts a conversation no longer keeps Space and the keys 1 to 9 taken.
- The camera no longer comes closer when a window opens during a fight; the weights window's Import works again; moved game windows keep their place between a city and the world.
