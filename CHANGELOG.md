# Wanderer 0.43.0

### New
- What you play: a list of the ways your class is played (a bear and a cat for a feral druid), chosen by you and kept for each character. The green arrow, the tooltips and the extensions judge your gear for it; nothing is guessed.
- Three looks of Wanderer's own, offered on a new step of the welcome screen and in the options: the minimap with soft edges, its icons apart and the zone, time and coordinates below it; the quest tracker in small golden capitals on a transparent ground; a quiet bar at the top of the screen (the menu, the reminders, your extensions' windows, the other addons' icons), which takes the minimap button's place.
- The Wanderer addons you do not have yet are offered in Wanderer's menu and on a step of the welcome screen; your extensions' news show with Wanderer's.

### Changed
- An item is "better" only in your class's best armor type: cloth is never better for one who wears leather.
- A weight changed, what you play or your talents: the comparisons are judged again at once, in Wanderer and its extensions, without a reload.
- Quest levels in the colors of your quest log.
- Every action in Wanderer's windows is a real button.
- The ways to play of the welcome screen go further: the immersive ones also make the profiles by place (all the interface and the damage meter in dungeons, a quiet city), the dungeons' one shows the damage meter, and the minimap in Wanderer's style comes with each of them.
- Wanderer's Edit Mode layouts set the game's damage meter on the right of the action bars, over the bags.
- Lighter: only your language is loaded; the quest tracker and the minimap follow the game's events rather than timers.

### Fixed
- With a two-handed weapon, an off-hand item is no longer said to be better, and a one-hander is weighed against the two-hander alone.
- Windows behave as on a desktop: none is pushed under another or hidden behind the map any more, with a title bar or not, the game's or another addon's; the last opened or clicked comes in front.
